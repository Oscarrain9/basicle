About Us

Midland Appliance is a locally owned company based in British Columbia, Canada. Founded in 1984, the company has built a strong reputation for providing high-quality home appliances and exceptional customer service.

Our Products

We offer a wide selection of kitchen and home appliances, including refrigerators, ovens, dishwashers, washers, and dryers. Our showrooms feature products from over 50 leading global brands.
